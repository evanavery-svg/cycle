Apple Health 🍎

Move your cycle data to and from Apple Health.

Import: in the Health app, tap your photo → Export All Health Data, unzip it, and choose export.xml here. We'll read your Cycle Tracking (period & sexual activity) records.

Export: creates an Apple Health–format file. Because Apple doesn't let web apps write to Health directly, bring it in with the “Import Health Data” Shortcut on iOS.

Your body. Your data. 🔒

Welcome to cycle. Before you begin, here's our promise about your privacy — because period and health data is some of the most personal data there is.

  • It lives on your phone. Everything you log — periods, symptoms, moods, intimacy, medications — is stored locally on this device, in this app. Full stop.
  • No account, no cloud. There's no sign-up and no server. We never see your data because it never leaves your device.
  • No tracking, no ads, no selling. There are zero analytics, trackers, or third-party advertisers. Your cycle is nobody's business but yours.
  • You're in control. Export a copy anytime, or erase everything with one tap in Settings.
  • One thing to know: because data is stored only here, clearing your browser/app data or deleting the app removes it. Use Export to keep a backup.

cycle is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice.
